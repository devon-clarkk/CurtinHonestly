## The angle

Plan section 2 fixes the tagline ("Authentic anonymous reviews from Curtin
students"), the standing line and the one-sentence pitch. Nothing here changes
them. The seasonal hook on top is **rant or rave**: you just finished the unit,
say what it was actually like, rate it, rant about it or recommend it. The
ranting energy lives in Discord posts, stories and hooks. The site's own banner
and the posters stay in the plan's register: short, plain, second person, the
limitation said out loud.

No invented reviews anywhere. Poster 01 uses a written-for-the-poster review and
says so in its spec; poster 02 shows the real empty state; the new poster 04
shows the real review form with every field empty.

## Channels, in the plan's order

1. Unit coordinators with the lecture slide (plan section 9). Highest yield, free. Emails go in the last two weeks of teaching, so by Fri 30 Oct.
2. r/Curtin, honest route only: modmail first, then posts A, B, C (plan section 6).
3. Student societies: ComSSA, Curtin Engineers Club, CDSA, Robotics, each with a `club-<slug>` link and the G2 partnership draw (plan G2; society email in `copy-additions-2026-s2.md`).
4. Posters 02 and 03 in exam fortnight, 01 held for February, 04 for results day. Flyer A at the library and any stall. Stickers wait for O-Week.
5. Devon's LinkedIn (posts-drafts.md, with post 1 updated) and Instagram stories with the four 9:16 cuts. The company page kit is credibility infrastructure, not a channel (linkedin/README.md).
6. Student media only if the numbers earn it in December (press note in the copy additions).

## Budget

Recommended shape for this campaign: AUD 550 to 690 (G1 five $50 vouchers, G2
$150 club prize, posters and flyers). Lean shape: 320 to 380 (G1 only, a small
print run). Adding the results-week coffee stall (G3) and stickers takes it to
about 1,000, which is the plan's section 12 figure. Itemised in
`giveaway-plan-2026-s2.md`.

# CurtinHonestly LinkedIn copy

Australian English. No em dashes. Voice and positioning taken from
`design/marketing/launch-plan.md` section 2, so this reads as the same brand as the
posters, the video, and the Reddit drafts.

Nothing below claims a follower count, a review count, a user count, or any other
number the site cannot currently prove.

---

## 1. Tagline

LinkedIn company page tagline field. Limit 120 characters.

**Use this one.**

```
Honest, anonymous unit reviews written by Curtin students. Independent, and not affiliated with Curtin University.
```

It does two jobs in one line: it says what the site is, and it answers the first
question anyone asks, which is whether this is an official University page.

**Alternates, same limit.**

```
Authentic anonymous reviews from Curtin students. Ratings, workload, and would-take-again scores.
```

The brand tagline with the product fields attached. Use it if the disclaimer is
already obvious elsewhere on the page.

```
Independent student platform. Honest reviews of Curtin units, written anonymously by the students who took them.
```

Leads with independence. Use it if the page starts attracting "is this official?"
comments.

## 2. About

LinkedIn company description field. Limit 2000 characters.

```
CurtinHonestly is an independent platform where Curtin University students write honest reviews of the units they have taken.

Every unit page carries a rating out of five, a workload score out of ten, whether there was an exam, whether the reviewer would take the unit again, and an optional final grade. You can compare units side by side before you enrol.

Who it is for

Students choosing units. The handbook tells you what a unit covers. It does not tell you that the group assignment is worth 40% and the groups are allocated randomly, or that the exam looks nothing like the tutorials. The students who sat it know all of that, and it gets lost in group chats every semester.

Why it exists

Choosing units has real consequences and almost no reliable information behind it. Asking around only works if you happen to know someone who did the unit. A Curtin student built this so you do not have to rely on that.

What anonymous means here

Your name and email are never shown next to a review. Other students see only whether the reviewer is a verified student. Reviews are still linked to your account in our database, which is what lets you edit and delete your own reviews, stops the same account reviewing a unit twice, and lets us investigate reports of abuse. We would rather say that plainly than let you find it out later. Verify with a student.curtin.edu.au address and your reviews carry a verified badge.

Independence

CurtinHonestly is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Curtin University. Nobody at the University approves what is published, and nobody there can take a review down for being unflattering. Reviews are removed for abuse or for breaking the terms, not for being negative. There are no ads and nothing to buy.

The site is new and the catalogue is still filling up. If you have finished a unit, writing about it takes about ninety seconds, and it is the thing that makes the next student's decision a better one.

curtinhonestly.com
```

## 3. Specialties and keywords

LinkedIn allows up to 20 specialties on a company page. These are the 15 worth using,
ordered by how likely someone is to search them.

```
Student unit reviews
Course reviews
Curtin University units
Unit selection
Elective selection
Student feedback
Anonymous reviews
Higher education
Student experience
Peer reviews
Course selection
Workload transparency
University enrolment
Independent student platform
Education technology
```

**Note on "course" versus "unit".** The About copy says *unit*, because that is what
Curtin calls them and voice rule 7 in the launch plan is to use the student's own
language. *Course reviews* and *course selection* still appear in the specialties list,
because that is what people outside Curtin type into a search box. Keywords are for the
search index, body copy is for the reader. Do not let the keyword leak back into the
About text.

## 5. Copy rules for anything added later

Same rules as the rest of the campaign, repeated here so this folder stands alone.

1. No em dashes.
2. No invented numbers. If the site cannot show it, do not write it.
3. Say the limitation out loud. The anonymity paragraph above is the model.
4. "Independent student platform. Not affiliated with Curtin University." appears
   somewhere on every public surface.
5. Australian English. Enrol, organise, colour, semester, unit.
6. Never claim to be Curtin, never position against Curtin. Independent is a fact
   stated calmly, not a grievance.

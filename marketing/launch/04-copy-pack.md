## 4. r/Curtin post (Wed 25 Nov), with disclosure

**Title:** I built an anonymous unit review site for Curtin. Semester's done, tell it what your units were actually like.

**Body:**

Disclosure up front: I'm a Curtin computing student and I built this.

CurtinHonestly (https://www.curtinhonestly.com/?ref=rcurtin) is an independent review site covering all 1,761 Curtin units across the five faculties. For a unit you've finished you rate it, rate the workload, say whether you'd take it again, and optionally add the grade you got. Reviews are anonymous. Accounts verified with a student email get a "verified student" label, but anyone can post.

It is thin: under 50 reviews, almost all in Science and Engineering. I'm posting now because the fortnight after exams is the only time anyone remembers what a unit was really like, and because every page you click on will probably be empty. If yours is, be the first.

There's a prize draw running until 12 Dec for verified students who write a qualifying review (terms on the site; [prize]).

Moderation rules, since this is Reddit: staff can be named about their teaching of the unit, nothing about their private lives, appearance or anything protected. Reviews get removed for that and the draw entry goes with them.

Happy to answer anything about how it works, what's stored, or the code (it's public on GitHub).

Not affiliated with Curtin University.
